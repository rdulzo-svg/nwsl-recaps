Washington Spirit hold the top spot for a second straight week, their 1-0 win over expansion side Denver Summit FC enough to edge the rating slightly higher while Gotham FC and Kansas City Current stayed within shouting distance behind them.

The attack and defense splits tell a sharper story than the overall rating alone: Gotham FC's number two ranking is carried almost entirely by the league's best defense, not its attack, which sits barely above average. Kansas City Current is the mirror image near the top — the league's most productive attack paired with a defense that's given up more than the rating alone suggests, which is part of why a rough patch dropped them five spots' worth of rating this week despite the raw firepower.

The biggest movers both came out of the same result: Utah Royals FC jumped to fifth after hanging four goals on North Carolina Courage, while the Courage dropped to 11th on the other end of that scoreline — the single largest swing of the week in either direction.

At the bottom, Chicago Stars FC remain in last with the league's worst attack and worst defense alike, their 1-0 loss to Orlando Pride doing little to change a season that's already produced 12 defeats.

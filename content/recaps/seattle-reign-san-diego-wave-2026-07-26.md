San Diego controlled the match by the numbers — 61% possession, a 22-9 shot advantage — but Seattle made its two real chances count, and goalkeeper Claudia Dickey turned away the other 22 shots to preserve the result.

Ainsley McCammon set up both Reign goals: first lofting a ball into the box in the 17th minute for Maddie Mercado to head home, then setting up Jordyn Bugg's header off a corner three minutes later to make it 2-0. The two assists made McCammon the youngest player in NWSL history to record multiple assists in a single game, breaking a record Trinity Rodman set in 2021.

The win was also a milestone for Seattle head coach Laura Harvey, who picked up her 100th career win with the club — the first coach in NWSL history to reach 100 wins with a single franchise.

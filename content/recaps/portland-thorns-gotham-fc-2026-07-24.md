Portland's 11-match unbeaten run at home stayed intact Friday night, but it took two separate comebacks to get there. Gotham FC left Providence Park with a share of the points after a back-and-forth 2-2 draw in front of 21,743 fans.

Gotham struck first — and fast. Midge Purce needed just two minutes to put her team ahead, burying a volley in the box before the home crowd had settled in.

Portland's response came from an unlikely source. Rookie midfielder Renée Lyles, making her first NWSL start, danced around her defender and chipped the ball into the far-right corner for her first professional goal — leveling the match and announcing herself in the same motion.

The Thorns took the lead for the first time in the 53rd minute, when Jessie Fleming finished to put Portland ahead 2-1. It didn't last. Nine minutes later, Sam Kerr — scoring the 78th regular-season goal of her career — pulled Gotham level again, and the match would finish 2-2.

The draw arrived alongside the long-awaited return of forward Morgan Weaver, back in the Portland lineup after time away from the club.

Neither side could find a winner in the closing stages, and the point extends Portland's home unbeaten streak to 11 straight — a new club record — while denying Gotham what would have been a statement road win.

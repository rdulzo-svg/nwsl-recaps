Aïssata Traoré needed a bit of luck, and Boston Legacy FC made it count. In the 18th minute, a corner kick deflected off Kansas City's Bianca St-Georges and fell to Traoré, who buried a low strike into the back of the net for the match's only goal.

Kansas City pushed for an equalizer for the rest of the match but couldn't find a way through, leaving Boston with its third shutout in four games. The result drops Kansas City (8-7-1, 25 points) to sixth in the standings, while Boston (5-8-4, 19 points) moves up to 12th.

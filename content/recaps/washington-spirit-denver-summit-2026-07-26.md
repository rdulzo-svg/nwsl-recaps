Washington Spirit's defense did the heavy lifting for most of the night against expansion side Denver Summit FC, but it was Sofia Cantore who delivered the difference in the 61st minute — taking a pass from Trinity Rodman and firing a shot from distance that clipped the underside of the bar on its way in.

It was Cantore's eighth league goal of the season, and her fourth from outside the box. Denver had chances of its own — Yazmeen Ryan's 12th-minute effort came agonizingly close, just missing off the crossbar — but Washington's defense and goalkeeper Sandy MacIver held firm.

The shutout was Washington's seventh of the season and the club's tenth win in its last 12 league matches. At 33 points through 16 matches, the Spirit sit two points clear at the top of the table.

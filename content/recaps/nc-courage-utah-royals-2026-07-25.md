Utah Royals FC put together the most complete performance of their club history, scoring four goals on the road to run away with a 4-1 win over North Carolina Courage.

Kate Del Fava opened the scoring just six minutes in, heading home a corner kick delivered by Nuria Rábano. Tatumn Milazzo doubled the lead in the 35th minute with a left-footed finish from inside the box, and Mina Tanaka made it three in the 65th minute, heading in a cross from Cloé Lacasse. Lacasse herself was also among Utah's scorers on the night.

North Carolina's lone goal came from Evelyn Ijeh, who capitalized after Shinomi Koyama forced a turnover on the edge of Utah's box. It wasn't enough to slow down a Utah side that put together its highest-scoring performance in franchise history.

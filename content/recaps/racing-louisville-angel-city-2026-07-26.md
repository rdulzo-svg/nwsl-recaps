Angel City controlled the opening 45 minutes on Pride Night at BMO Stadium — 67% possession, an 11-8 shot advantage — but it was Racing Louisville making the most of its chances on the counter.

Rookie forward Maja Lardner opened the scoring in the 24th minute, then doubled the lead in the 55th with a header off a Kayla Fischer cross — the first two goals of her professional career, both coming in the same match. Angel City's Sveindís Jónsdóttir pulled one back in the 80th minute, but the rally came too late, and Racing held on for a 2-1 road win.

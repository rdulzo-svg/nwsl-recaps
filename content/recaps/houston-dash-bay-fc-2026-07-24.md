Ninety minutes of scoreless, defense-first soccer at Shell Energy Stadium looked headed for a draw — until Linda Ullmark turned inside the box in second-half stoppage time and fired a shot into the near post, sending the home crowd into a frenzy and Bay FC home with nothing.

The rookie's second goal of the season came off an assist from defender Avery Patterson, her first of the year. It marked Houston's third stoppage-time game-winner of the campaign and ended Bay FC's unbeaten run at three matches.

For a Dash side leaning on rookie production all season, the goal was fitting: the 10th scored by a Houston rookie in 2026.

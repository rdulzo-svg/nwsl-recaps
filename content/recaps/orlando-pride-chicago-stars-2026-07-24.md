Orlando dominated the run of play for most of the night — 20 of the team's 28 shots came in the first half alone — but it took until the 88th minute to find the breakthrough against a stubborn Chicago Stars defense.

Cori Dyke slid a weighted through ball into the box for Julie Doyle, who finished left-footed into the upper corner for her first goal of the season. It was Dyke's first assist of the year.

Chicago goalkeeper Katie Atkinson kept the match scoreless deep into the second half, finishing with eight saves, but couldn't come up with a ninth when it mattered most. The result gave Orlando its sixth clean sheet of the season, third-most in the NWSL.
